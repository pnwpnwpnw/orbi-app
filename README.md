# Orbi

A small white bot that lives in the corner of your Mac's screen, so you can chat with [Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code) without opening a terminal.

**Download:** [latest release](../../releases/latest)

## What it does
- **Claude's permission questions, answered in Orbi (version 0.6.1 and newer).** When a chat you started in Orbi needs your OK ("Claude wants to Run: ls -la"), a small card shows **Allow**, **Always ▾** and **Deny**, Orbi chimes and bounces, and the chat window opens if it was hidden. **Always** (0.6.2) opens a menu that names the rule ("Always allow Bash(ls:*)") and lets you choose how long it lasts: **This session only**, **This folder (saved)** or **Everywhere (saved)**. Orbi only passes your choice to Claude Code, which does the saving.
- **Claude app Code conversations inside Orbi (0.6.1).** The title menu has a **Claude Code** section: pick a conversation from the Claude app and its history opens in Orbi, with the pictures you sent shown as thumbnails and any turn run elsewhere appearing live. What you type there goes to the same conversation. If the Claude app already shows it (even behind other windows) and its message box is empty, Orbi types it in for you **in the background**, without bringing the app forward, so it appears there too (needs Orbi's Accessibility permission, see [below](#send-messages-to-the-claude-app-experimental)); otherwise Orbi runs the turn itself, and the Claude app shows it after it reloads that conversation. Orbi never sends while the Claude app is working in that conversation, and never twice.
- **Your Claude account, at a glance (0.6.3).** Orbi and the Claude app sign in separately, so a limit reached on one can look like a broken Orbi. **Settings → General → Claude account** shows who Orbi's `claude` is signed in as, with **Sign in…** / **Switch account…** (opens Terminal on `claude auth login`; you sign in there in your browser) and **Sign out…** (asks first, because it signs `claude` out everywhere on your Mac). If Orbi finds it signed out, he says so in his bubble and a click starts the sign-in.
- **Watches Chat too (version 0.5.0 and newer).** The Claude app's **Chat** view (the speech-bubble icon in its sidebar; this is where Cowork and Chat now live together) keeps nothing on your Mac that Orbi could read, so Orbi reads the window itself. While Claude answers there, Orbi thinks and the bubble says "Claude is answering in …", then shows each step ("Edited a file, created a file, and 12 more steps"); when it ends he hops, chimes and says "Finished in … · click to open". A click brings the Claude app to that conversation, even from the Code view. Needs **Watch Claude app** and Orbi's Accessibility permission (see [below](#send-messages-to-the-claude-app-experimental)). Orbi can only see the view that is on screen: while the Claude app shows Code, he keeps what he last saw for a few minutes.
- **Send to Chat (version 0.5.0 and newer).** The conversation button in the quick-ask box now has a **Chat** section: *New chat*, *Whatever Chat shows* (continues the last one) and your recent Chat conversations. Orbi switches the Claude app to Chat for a moment (and opens its sidebar if you keep it folded, then folds it again), types your message and goes back to what you were doing. A message for Chat waits while Chat is still answering, like the ones for Code.
- **Split view fix (0.5.0).** Right after Orbi woke the Claude app, a split view could briefly show only one message box and Orbi took it for the only pane. He now waits for the window to settle and checks the folder above the box before typing.
- **Control + Shift + Space** does what clicking Orbi does: his input box opens beside him, from any app; press it again to close (version 0.4.7 and newer; change it in Settings under *Open Orbi's box*). It avoids ⌃Space (macOS keyboard-language switch) and ⌥Space (used by several other apps).
- **Control + Command + Space** opens a small quick-ask bar from any app: Orbi, an input box, the model and the folder. Type, press Return, and the answer shows in Orbi's speech bubble. The small chat button in the bar lets you choose which chat the message goes to: a new one, or one of your 5 latest. You can change the shortcut in Settings.
- **Click Orbi** to ask Claude something from the desktop. A speech bubble shows what Claude is doing, and short replies appear right there.
- **Click the menu-bar icon** to open the full chat window, with your saved conversations, a light or dark look that follows your Mac, and replies that appear as Claude writes them, formatted (bold, lists, code blocks, links). The steps Claude takes ("Read Sessions.swift") stay in the chat as small pills. Hover a chat in the title menu and click its trash icon to delete it. While the window is open, the Orbi from your desktop glides over and sits in its top-left corner (right-click him there for the quick settings), then goes back when it closes; from 0.6.4 he stays in view when a menu opens. A very long message you paste shows only its first 4,000 characters in its bubble (0.6.5), so the window stays smooth; Claude still gets all of it. Click anywhere else and the window goes away.
- **Orbi acts out what Claude is doing:** it scans while reading, taps while editing, peers around while searching, hunkers down while running a command and bounces while writing the reply.
- **Drag a file or photo onto Orbi** and it eats it. The file goes along with your next message.
- Pick the model (Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Haiku) from the label next to the input.
- Orbi walks around your screen. Click it and use the Move / Stay pill to turn that off.
- **Right-click Orbi** and a fan of quick settings swings out beside it, three pills at a time (scroll with a trackpad, mouse wheel or the arrow keys for the rest; the pill you click glides to the middle): **Send to Claude app**, **Show name**, **Token bar**, **Watch Claude app**, **Helper agents**, **Stop Claude**, **Permission alerts**, **Sounds**, **Auto updates** (click to switch on or off; orange is on for the Claude ones, white for the rest), **Model** (click to step through the models) and **Full settings…**. Each pill has a small icon, and Orbi glances toward them while they are open. In **Settings → Quick settings** you choose which pills show.
- **Usage meter (version 0.4.6 and newer).** A thin bar with a percentage sits above Orbi's name tag. By default it shows your Claude **session limit**, the same number as in the Claude app; click **Token bar** in the right-click fan and small pills fan out of it: **Show number**, **Show bar**, and a pill that cycles between **Session limit**, **Weekly limit** and the **conversation's context**. The bar turns orange from 70% and red from 90%. Orbi gets the limits by asking Claude itself (`claude -p "/usage"`, about every five minutes), so it never sees your login. It needs a Claude subscription (Pro or Max); the context option works for everyone who has **Watch Claude app** on.
- **Watch the Claude app (optional, version 0.4.5 and newer).** Switch on **Watch Claude app** and Orbi acts out what Claude is doing in your Claude app conversations: all the active ones, or only the one you pick in Settings, even with several working at once. The bubble shows the latest step, there is a hop and a chime when one finishes, and a click on the bubble jumps to that conversation. Orbi only reads each conversation's own log; it never writes to it.
- **Permission alerts (optional, version 0.4.5 and newer).** Orbi can speak up the moment Claude asks for your permission, with a chime, a bounce and what it wants to do, until you answer. Turn it on with the **Permission alerts** pill or in Settings. It asks first, then adds a small note to Claude's settings that only passes the request to Orbi; it can't approve, deny or delay anything, and your other settings are kept (a backup is saved next to the file). Turn it off any time.
- **Helper agents (version 0.4.7 and newer).** When Claude starts helpers (sub-agents), a small copy of Orbi pops out beside him for each one, bobs while it works, and hops and fades when it finishes. More than six become a "+N" badge, and a bubble says what the new helper does. Needs **Watch Claude app**; switch it off in Settings or with the **Helper agents** pill.
- **To-do progress, a summary of what was done, and background jobs (0.4.7).** While Claude works through a to-do list, "3/7" with a thin bar sits under Orbi. When a turn finishes, the bubble says what it did, like "Finished in “Fix login” · 3 files +42 −7 · 12 commands". When a command Claude left running in the background finishes, Orbi chimes and tells you whether it worked.
- **Warnings (0.4.7).** Orbi speaks up when your session or weekly limit reaches 80% and again at 95% (switch it off in Settings). If a turn ends in an error, Orbi shows what went wrong instead of cheering; he mentions it when Claude keeps retrying; and a turn that has been quiet for 3 minutes gets one gentle nudge. A turn you stop yourself no longer leaves Orbi thinking.
- **Messages that wait (0.4.7).** Typed a message for a Claude app conversation while Claude is still answering? Orbi holds it (a small number badge shows on him) and sends it when Claude has finished, instead of interrupting the answer. Click Orbi for *Send now*, *Cancel* or *Type another message*. Needs **Watch Claude app** and **Send to Claude app**; switch it off in Settings.
- **Commands and skills (0.4.7).** Type **/** in the quick-ask box or the Control + Command + Space bar and a list of your commands and skills opens (the Claude Code ones, your own, your plugins and the Claude app's). Up and Down choose, Return fills the name in (a second Return sends it).
- **Stop Claude (experimental, 0.4.7).** The **Stop Claude** pill in the right-click fan stops the answer being written in the conversation Orbi sees working, by pressing the stop button (or Escape) in the Claude app. Needs **Watch Claude app** and **Send to Claude app**. From version 0.5.1 it also stops a Chat answer: if Orbi sees Chat working (and Chat is where your messages go, or no Code conversation is busy), the pill shows that conversation and presses the stop button.
- **Quick settings in the quick-ask bar (0.4.7).** Press Control + Command + Space and the Orbi on your desktop glides over to the bar and takes the place beside the input; right-click him there for the same quick settings, straight instead of tilted. The bar also has a small expand button that opens the full chat window. The full chat window has a matching collapse button in its header that brings the small bar back. When the bar closes he glides back.
- **Sound effects (version 0.4.5 and newer).** Soft sounds for clicks, the quick-ask bar, sending, replies, errors, the right-click fan and eating. They are made inside the app, kept quiet, and switched off with the **Sounds** pill or in Settings.
- **Settings:** right-click the menu-bar icon and choose **Settings…**, or use **Full settings…** in the right-click fan (version 0.3.0 and newer; a new dark look in 0.4.5). Pick where Orbi parks, how fast it walks, its name, the shortcut, the folder new chats start in, sounds, watching the Claude app, permission alerts, which right-click pills show, and rename or delete saved conversations.
- **Send to the Claude app (experimental, version 0.3.0 and newer).** Type into a conversation open in the Claude desktop app, and pick which one. See [below](#send-messages-to-the-claude-app-experimental).

## Install
1. Download `Orbi-…-macOS-arm64.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and unzip it.
2. Move `Orbi.app` to `/Applications`.
3. The first time, **right-click the app, choose Open**, and click **Open**. If macOS still refuses, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. (The app isn't signed by an Apple developer account, so macOS asks once.)

## Send messages to the Claude app (experimental)

From version 0.3.0, Orbi can also type your message into the **Claude desktop app**, so it lands in a conversation you already have open there, as if you had typed it yourself. Replies show up in the Claude app, not in Orbi.

**Set it up (once)**
1. Install Orbi in your **Applications** folder and open it, and make sure the **Claude app** is installed and running.
2. Right-click Orbi itself and click the **Send to Claude app** pill, so it turns orange. (The same switch is in **Settings…**.) A dialog explains the permission; click **Open the permission prompt**.
3. macOS opens **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility**. Switch **Orbi** on. If it isn't in the list yet, click **"+"**, choose **Orbi** in your Applications folder and switch it on. macOS may ask for your password.
4. In the Claude app, open the conversation you want to talk to and leave its window open (not minimized).
5. **Test it:** press Control + Command + Space (or click Orbi), type "hello from Orbi" and press Enter. The Claude app flashes to the front, the message appears in the conversation, and you land back where you were.

If step 5 says Orbi isn't allowed to control other apps even though the switch looks on, see "Good to know" below.

**Use it**
- Click Orbi (or press the shortcut). The box now says "Send to the Claude app…", and a button shows where your message will go, like **"→ GitHub repo access ▾"**. Right-click Orbi and click the pill again to switch it off.
- Click that button to pick a different conversation from your recent ones. Orbi reads the titles from the Claude app's own session files and never changes them.
- Type your message and press Enter. Orbi brings the Claude app to the front, pastes the message, presses Return and takes you back to what you were doing. It takes about a second, so don't click or type in that moment. Your clipboard is put back afterwards.
- Dropped files are added to the message as file paths.

**Safe by design**
- If you picked a conversation and the Claude app isn't showing it, Orbi types **nothing** and tells you why.
- Orbi only types the messages you send it, and only into the Claude app.
- Switching to another conversation uses the Claude app's own link. It worked in testing, but a future version of the app could change that. If switching fails, Orbi stops and sends nothing.

**Good to know**
- The Claude app has to be running. Orbi doesn't open it for you.
- Orbi's Enter works like your own: if Claude is in the middle of an answer when you send, that answer is **interrupted** and your new message is handled instead. From version 0.4.7, with **Watch Claude app** on, Orbi waits for Claude to finish instead (see *Messages that wait* above); choose *Send now* from his menu if you do want to interrupt.
- If macOS says Orbi isn't allowed to control other apps, even though the switch looks on, the permission belongs to an older copy of the app. Select **Orbi** (or **Claude Bridge**, its old name) in the Accessibility list, press **"−"** to remove it, then add it again. From version 0.4.0 Orbi is signed the same way every time, so this should only be needed once, when you update from an older version.
- **Chat (0.5.0):** pick *New chat*, *Whatever Chat shows* or a conversation under **Chat** in the conversation button. The Claude app needs a window open; if its sidebar is folded Orbi opens it for a moment, because the Chat switch and your conversations live there.
- If the Claude app shows **several conversations side by side**, Orbi finds the right one by the folder shown above it. If it can't tell (say, two panes on the same folder), it types nothing and tells you why.
- It works by controlling a window, so a future change to the Claude app's layout could break it. **Save Claude app layout** in **Settings…** writes a description of the window that makes such a break easy to diagnose.

## You need
- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later. It does not run on Intel Macs.
- The `claude` command line tool, installed and logged in (`claude /login`). Orbi finds it on your PATH, in `~/.local/bin`, or in the Claude desktop app's bundled copy (which has to be logged in once). Not signed in yet (or on another account than the Claude app)? Use **Settings → General → Claude account** (0.6.3).

## Updates
From version 0.2.0 on, Orbi updates itself. About once a day it checks this page; when there is a newer version, Orbi says so in its speech bubble ("Orbi 0.2.1 is out. Click to update"). Click Orbi and it downloads the update, checks it, restarts and opens the new version. You can also use **Check now…** in **Settings…** (right-click the menu-bar icon), or switch the automatic check off there.

If you have version 0.1.0, install the latest release by hand once; it can't update itself. Your conversations and settings are kept.

## Privacy
No servers, no accounts, no tracking. Everything goes through the `claude` tool on your own Mac. The only thing Orbi contacts itself is GitHub, about once a day, to see whether a newer version exists. The usage meter asks the `claude` tool on your Mac for your limits. The account line in Settings asks it who is signed in (`claude auth status`, no password or token involved); signing in or out happens in Terminal, never inside Orbi. Watching the Claude app, the extras above and permission alerts stay on your Mac: Orbi reads the conversation logs Claude Code already keeps there (and, for the **/** list, the folders where your skills and plugins live, without changing them), and the optional permission note is a small file in `~/Library/Application Support/ClaudeBridge/`. Conversations are saved only on your Mac, in `~/Library/Application Support/ClaudeBridge/`.
